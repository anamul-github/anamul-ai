## How do I approach a problem I've never seen before?

When I run into a bug I haven't seen before, I first try to understand what's happening and reproduce the issue. I break the problem into smaller parts, check the logs and the relevant code, and narrow down where things are going wrong. Then I test possible explanations and verify that the fix solves the problem without breaking something else.

If I get stuck, I read the documentation, research similar issues, or ask a teammate with the right experience. When I ask for help, I explain what I've already tried, so we can move forward quickly.

With new tools, I'm naturally curious. I like experimenting and building small examples to understand how things work. I make mistakes along the way, but I keep going until that "aha" moment when things finally click. That's one of my favorite parts of working in technology.

I still care about the details, but now I put that effort where it makes the biggest difference.
