# How to Get Rich Without Luck: A Practical Guide to Financial Freedom

By Anamul Hasan, published 24 November, 2024. Category: Finance 🤑

#### Introduction:

Have you ever wondered if getting rich is something you can control?

For many people, financial freedom feels like a dream. Most people work their entire lives just to retire at 65 or 70. But the truth is, **getting rich isn’t about luck—it’s a skill anyone can learn**. I know this because I’m the guy who once bought a BMW with a credit card.

I’m also the same guy who spent years learning about money through books, podcasts, videos, blogs, articles, and conversations with people. I’ve read books like ***Rich Dad Poor Dad, The Psychology of Money, A Simple Path to Wealth, The Almanack of Naval Ravikant, Think & Grow Rich, The Millionaire Fastlane, The 4-Hour Workweek***, and many others. Over time, I discovered that building wealth is about making smart choices and staying consistent with good habits.

In this article, I’ll share the lessons I’ve learned and the strategies that truly work. Let’s dive in and explore how you can take control of your financial future—no luck required.

##### What Does It Mean to Be Rich?

Before diving into how to get rich, it’s important to ask yourself: What does “rich” really mean? For some people, being rich means having fancy cars, big houses, or lots of money in the bank. For others, it’s something much simpler—having the freedom to live life the way they want.

**Being rich is relative**—it depends on your goals and lifestyle. Wealth isn’t about how much money you have; it’s about the choices that money gives you. **True wealth is freedom**: freedom from financial stress, freedom to spend your time doing what you love, and freedom to live a joyful life.

Take a moment to think about what freedom means to you. Once you shift your mindset from chasing money to seeking freedom, you’ll find it easier to stay motivated and make better financial decisions. With this understanding, let’s explore the **two** main ways to achieve financial freedom.

1. The Traditional Way
2. The Fastlane Approach

#### 1. The Traditional Way

The traditional way to get rich is simple: spend less money than you make and save the rest. It’s one of the most reliable ways to build wealth over time. The results can be amazing:

1. **Save 50% of your income**: If you save half of what you earn starting at age 20, you could retire comfortably by 37.
2. **Save 75% of your incom**e: If you save 75% of your income, you only need to work for 7 years from now on.

This isn’t just an idea—it’s worked for many people. For example, ***Money Mustache*** couple saved about 66% of their income and, in just under ten years, they retired. Their money is working for them.

Saving half or more of your income might sound hard, but it’s possible with some changes in your lifestyle. Here are some simple tips to help you save more money:

##### Tips to Save Money

1. **Pay Off Debt**: Get rid of loans, especially ones with high interest.
2. **Live Close to Work**: Save on transportation costs/time by cutting down your commute.
3. **Move to a Cheaper City**: If you enjoy adventure, consider living in a place with a lower cost of living.
4. **Avoid Borrowing for Cars**: Avoid taking car loans—buy only what you can afford.
5. **Skip Expensive Cars**: A simple, reliable car is all you need.
6. **Ride a Bike**: It’s cheaper than driving and good for your health and also for the environment.
7. **Cancel Extra Subscriptions**: Cut out streaming services or other unused subscriptions.
8. **Spend Less on Groceries**: Plan meals and stick to a budget, like $80 a week for organic groceries for 2 people.
9. **Limit Dining Out**: Save restaurant visits for special occasions, not everyday meals.
10. **Avoid Ivy League Pre-school syndrome for Kids**: Your kids don’t need expensive preschools to succeed.
11. **Switch to a Cheaper Phone Plan**: Look for affordable options that still meet your needs.
12. **Do Things Yourself**: Learn to enjoy doing tasks on your own instead of paying for help.
13. **Stay Positive**: A good attitude helps you stay focused on your goals.

##### What Should You Do With the Money You Save?

Every paycheck you save is a step toward financial freedom. To grow your money, consider these simple options:

1. **Stock Index Funds**: Low-cost investments that grow steadily over time.
2. **Pay Off Your House Mortgage**: Owning your home means fewer monthly expenses.
3. **Buy Rental Property**: Renting out a home can give you extra income/cash flow.
4. **Keep Learning**: Read books and watch videos to learn how to make your money work for you.

##### How Long Will Your Money Last? The 4% Rule Explained

If you save and invest enough to have 25 times your yearly expenses, you can live off that money forever. This is where the 4% rule comes in—it’s a simple way to figure out how much you can safely withdraw each year without running out of money.

Here’s how it works:

1. **Save 25 Times Your Annual Expenses**

a) Calculate how much money you need to live for one year, then multiply that number by 25 to find your savings goal.

b) For example, **if you need $35,000 a year to live comfortably, your target savings is $875,000 ($35,000 × 25)**.

2. **Withdraw 4% Every Year**

a) Once you’ve reached your goal, you can withdraw 4% each year to cover your expenses.

b) In this example, **4% of $875,000 is $35,000 per year**.

##### Why It Works

Your savings are invested in assets like stock index funds, which have historically grown by 7–8% per year. After accounting for **inflation** (about 3%), your money still grows by 4–5%. Since you’re only withdrawing 4%, the rest of your savings stays invested, helping your money to keep growing or remain steady over time.

##### Let Your Money Work for You

As long as you follow the 4% rule, your investments will generate enough money to cover your expenses indefinitely. For example, **if you save $1 million, you can withdraw $40,000 a year to live on while your remaining money continues to grow**.

This way of getting rich isn’t just about numbers—it’s about changing how you live. Your mindset about money matters more than how much you know about finance. Stay disciplined, focus on your goals, and you’ll transform your life for the better.

By saving and investing early, you give your money the power to work for you. The 4% rule gives you the freedom to live without financial stress, so you can choose to work only if you want to.

#### 2. The Fastlane Approach

The traditional path to wealth can take decades, but there is another way—the Fastlane. The Fastlane Approach is about using **leverage, specific knowledge, and accountability** to create scalable opportunities that can generate wealth faster. Let’s dive into the key principles that make up this approach.

##### 1. Understand the Fastlane Mindset

Building wealth isn’t about luck; it’s a skill you learn. Wealth is created by understanding what to do, who to do it with, and when to act. Hard work matters, but it’s not enough on its own. You need to work on the right things with the right people.

> ***Making money is not a thing you do; it’s a skill you learn.***

The Fastlane mindset is about focusing on creating assets that earn while you sleep. This could be a business, a product, or intellectual property. The goal is to stop trading time for money and start creating systems that work for you.

##### 2. Create Wealth, Not Just Income

If you’re paid only for your time, your earnings are limited. To achieve financial freedom, you need to own equity—a piece of a business, product, or investment. This allows you to generate passive income, freeing you from the constraints of hourly work.

> ***You’re not going to get rich renting out your time.***

Ownership gives you the ability to earn even when you’re not actively working. Whether it’s starting your own business, owning stock options, or investing in scalable opportunities, equity is the key to escaping the time-for-money trap.

##### 3. Leverage is the Key to Wealth

Leverage multiplies your efforts and accelerates wealth creation. There are three main types of leverage:

1. **Labor**: Other people working for you.
2. **Capital**: Money that multiplies your decisions.
3. **Permissionless Products**: Scalable assets like **code**, **media**, or **intellectual property**.

The most powerful form of leverage is permissionless. You don’t need anyone’s approval to create software, write a book, or produce a video. These assets work for you 24/7 and can reach millions of people at little to no extra cost.

> ***Code and media are the new levers of wealth creation. They work while you sleep.***

##### 4. Find Specific Knowledge

Specific knowledge is unique to you—it’s something you can’t be trained for but can develop through your passions and curiosity. This might be sales skills, creative abilities, or technical expertise.

> ***No one can compete with you on being you.***

Pursue what feels like play to you but looks like work to others. This kind of knowledge is hard to automate or outsource, making it invaluable. **The internet has broadened opportunities for niche interests, allowing you to monetize your unique skills at scale.**

##### 5. Apply Accountability and Judgement

Taking ownership of your work and decisions is essential. Accountability builds credibility and leads to rewards like equity and leverage. It’s about taking risks under your own name and being responsible for the outcomes.

> ***You will get paid for your judgment, not just your work.***

Good judgment, paired with specific knowledge and leverage, is a multiplier for wealth creation. To develop this, focus on foundational skills like psychology, persuasion, and decision-making.

##### 6. Play Positive-Sum Games

Wealth creation is a positive-sum game—when you create value for others, you gain wealth in return. Avoid status games, which are zero-sum and rely on tearing others down to elevate yourself.

> ***Wealth creation is a positive-sum game; status is not.***

**Focus on building products, services, or solutions that society wants but doesn’t yet know how to get.** This mindset helps you create long-term value and build lasting wealth.

##### 7. Find Work That Feels Like Play

The best work aligns with your passions and talents. **When you love what you do, it doesn’t feel like work.** This allows you to put in the hours necessary to succeed without burning out.

> ***I’m always working, but it feels like play to me.***

Authenticity is key. Build businesses or products that reflect who you are and what you love. When your work is an extension of yourself, it’s hard for anyone to compete with you.

##### 8. Get Lucky the Right Way

Luck isn’t entirely random. There are **four** types of luck:

1. **Blind Luck**: Random events beyond your control.
2. **Luck Through Hustle**: Creating opportunities through hard work and persistence.
3. **Luck Through Preparation**: Developing skills and awareness to recognize opportunities others miss.
4. **Luck Through Character**: Becoming so good at what you do that opportunities find you.

> ***In a long-term game, everybody makes each other rich. In a short-term game, everyone is out for themselves.***

Focus on preparation and mastery to attract the right opportunities and maximize your chances of success.

##### 9. Be Patient, Stay Persistent

Wealth creation takes time and consistent effort. Patience is essential—there are no shortcuts. Focus on doing meaningful work and letting compound interest, both financial and personal, work in your favor.

> ***Apply specific knowledge with leverage, and eventually, you will get what you deserve.***

While making money is a fine goal, it’s not the end-all. The real value of wealth lies in the freedom it provides. Use it to solve external problems, but remember that **happiness, health, and fulfillment** come from within.

#### Conclusion

Getting rich is not about luck or shortcuts—it’s about learning the right skills, making smart choices, and staying consistent. Whether you follow the Traditional Way or take the Fastlane Approach, the key is to focus on what truly matters: **creating value, leveraging your strengths, and working toward financial freedom**.

Wealth isn’t just about money; it’s about having the freedom to live life on your own terms. By applying the principles shared in this article—**saving diligently, investing wisely, and building scalable opportunities**—you can set yourself on the path to a richer, more fulfilling life.

Start today, stay patient, and remember: the journey to financial freedom is as rewarding as the destination. With the right mindset and persistence, you have the power to create the life you’ve always wanted.
